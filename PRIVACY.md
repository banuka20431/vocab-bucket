Privacy Policy for Vocab-Bucket
Effective Date: May 23, 2026

1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy outlines how the "Vocab-Bucket" Chrome Extension ("we", "our", or "the extension") handles information when you install and use it. Vocab-Bucket is built as a non-commercial, single-purpose educational tool designed to help users define and save vocabulary words locally. Your privacy is our primary concern; Vocab-Bucket does not track, collect, sell, or distribute your personal data, browsing history, or user analytics.

2. Information We Do Not Collect
Vocab-Bucket operates entirely without user accounts. We do not require you to sign up, and we do not collect:

Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as names, addresses, or phone numbers.

Your web browsing history, URLs visited, or web page content (other than the specific single word you intentionally highlight to define).

Analytics, telemetry, or user interaction data.

3. How We Use Chrome Permissions
To function properly, Vocab-Bucket requests specific browser permissions. Here is exactly how they are used:

storage: Used exclusively to save your highlighted words and their definitions to your browser's local storage. This allows you to build a personal dictionary that persists across browsing sessions.

downloads: Used strictly to allow you to export and save a copy of your personal vocabulary list directly to your local device.

activeTab / Context Permissions: Used solely to read the specific word you have highlighted on the active webpage when you trigger the extension via the keyboard shortcut or menu.

4. Data Transmission and Third-Party Services
Vocab-Bucket requires an internet connection to fetch accurate dictionary definitions.

Data in Transit: When you highlight a word and request a definition, only that specific text string is securely transmitted to our custom backend server.

Backend Processing: Our backend securely routes the requested word to the Merriam-Webster Dictionary API to retrieve the definition.

No Context Sent: No metadata, IP addresses, personal identifiers, or information about the webpage you are currently reading is sent alongside this request. The backend server does not log or store the words you search for.

5. Data Storage and Security
All vocabulary words, definitions, and user preferences are stored entirely locally on your device using Chrome's chrome.storage.local API. This data never leaves your computer, is never synced to external cloud databases, and is only accessible by you. Because the data is stored locally, you have complete control over it and can delete your saved words or clear your storage at any time through the extension's interface.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in our extension's functionality or to comply with Web Store regulations. Any updates will be reflected by the "Effective Date" at the top of this document.
